Capabilities

What we build,
and maintain.

Full-lifecycle web work for organizations and the public sector, delivered to Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards and built to last.

i.

Website design and development

Hand-coded, mobile-first websites built for speed, stability, and long-term ownership. No bloated page builders, no recurring license fees baked into the foundation.

  • Responsive layouts tested across devices
  • Performance and Core Web Vitals tuning
  • Clean, standards-based markup
  • Hosting and deployment setup
ii.

Accessibility and Section 508 compliance

Public-facing sites designed to meet Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We can build to standard from the start or audit and remediate an existing site you already own.

  • WCAG 2.1 AA design and build
  • Accessibility audits with documented findings
  • Remediation of existing sites
  • Keyboard and screen-reader testing
iii.

Redesign and modernization

Migration of dated, fragile, or unmaintained sites onto a secure, current foundation, preserving the content and search equity you have already earned.

  • Content and structure migration
  • SEO preservation and redirects
  • Security and performance hardening
  • Modern, accessible rebuild
iv.

Maintenance and support

Ongoing care so the site stays fast, secure, and compliant after launch. Structured to fit how your organization procures recurring services.

  • Content updates and edits
  • Security patching and uptime monitoring
  • Accessibility monitoring
  • Priority support
v.

Search and digital presence

The technical and local groundwork that helps the right people find you, built on clean structure rather than gimmicks.

  • Technical and on-page SEO
  • Structured data and schema
  • Google Business Profile setup
  • Local search optimization
vi.

Brand and graphic design

Identity and layout work that keeps your digital and printed materials consistent and professional.

  • Logo and identity systems
  • Layout and document design
  • Capability statements and one-pagers
  • Digital collateral

Engagements scoped to the work

Projects are quoted by scope, not from a fixed price menu. Tell us what you need and we return a clear proposal with timeline and deliverables. For public-sector buyers, we work within your procurement process and provide a capability statement on request.
