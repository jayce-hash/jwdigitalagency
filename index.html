02
Accessibility & Compliance

Compliance is not a checkbox.
It is how we build.

Public-facing websites are held to a standard. Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act requires federal sites to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and the same expectation increasingly reaches state, local, and commercial work.

We design to that standard from the first line of code rather than retrofitting it at the end. This site is built the same way: keyboard navigable, screen-reader friendly, contrast-checked, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.

Perceivable 01

Text alternatives, sufficient color contrast, and content that adapts to assistive technology.

Operable 02

Full keyboard access, visible focus, and no motion that runs without the user's consent.

Understandable 03

Plain language, predictable navigation, and forms that explain what is required and why.

Robust 04

Clean, standards-based markup that works across browsers, devices, and screen readers.
